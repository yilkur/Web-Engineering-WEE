Welcome to the VIBE Application!
VIBE stands for: "Vitality, Intake, Body, Exercise"

The Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body weight relative to height. It is used to estimate whether a person is underweight, normal weight, overweight or obese. The BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by the square of height in meters:

BMI = weight in kg ÷ (height in m)2
